Fastmark — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Fastmark is an intermittent-fasting timer for Garmin watches, published by Small Hours Apps (smallhourslabs@gmail.com).

What Fastmark stores

Your running fast, your saved fasts (start, end and target) and your settings. All of this is stored only on your watch, plus the settings Garmin syncs through the Garmin Connect / Connect IQ app.

What Fastmark collects

Nothing. Fastmark makes no network requests, uses no sensors or location, has no analytics or ads, and sends no data to the publisher or any third party. The "Hours fasted" complication is shown only by watch faces on your own watch.

Deleting your data

Delete single fasts in the app's history, or uninstall Fastmark from your watch to remove all of its data.

Health note

Fastmark is a timer, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before fasting.
